A GUIDE TO LOOKING MORE CLOSELY

01 — THE BASICS

A gallery is more than four walls.

An art gallery is a place where art is shown, encountered and discussed. It might care for a collection, introduce emerging artists, sell work, or bring an exhibition to anyone with an internet connection.

01

Public

Community-facing spaces that make exhibitions and cultural programs accessible to everyone.

02

Commercial

Artist-led businesses that exhibit and sell work, building long-term relationships with collectors.

03

Museum

Institutions that preserve collections and place art in historical and cultural context.

04

Virtual

Digital exhibitions that expand access beyond the limits of geography and opening hours.

02 — AROUND THE WORLD

Different cities, different ways of seeing.

London, United Kingdom

Tate Modern

A former power station transformed into a home for international modern and contemporary art.

Paris, France

Musée du Louvre

A vast collection spanning thousands of years and cultures.

New York, USA

The Met

Art and objects that tell connected stories across time.

03 — WHY THEY MATTER

Room for a new point of view.

Galleries preserve shared histories while making space for ideas that are still taking shape. They support artists, invite public conversation and give people a reason to pause, wonder and see the familiar differently.

That work happens at every scale: in a national museum, a neighborhood project space and an online exhibition alike.
